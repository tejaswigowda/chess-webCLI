How this works (more)
  • The invite secret lives only after the # in the link; browsers never send it to a server.
  • Every move is signed by the player who made it and checked by every browser in the room against the rules. A modified page cannot move for you, or make an illegal move.
  • Introductions go through public Nostr relays. They see a room hash and encrypted connection data, never the moves.
  • The computer opponent is a small search engine in this page, not a model. The optional AI is a small language model that runs in your browser, and only turns your words into one of the legal moves, which you then confirm.
  • A room exists only while someone is in it. Nothing is stored anywhere but this tab.
